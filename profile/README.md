# Cleanverse

**Where Clean Money Meets Clean Hands.**

Building the foundations for trusted on-chain finance and AI-enabled commerce.

Cleanverse brings verified identity, verified assets, and programmable governance into a shared framework for digital value transfer. We connect institutional trust with blockchain infrastructure to help developers, financial institutions, and ecosystem partners build more accountable financial applications.

## Trust at the Foundation

On-chain finance needs clear answers to three questions: who is transacting, where the assets come from, and which rules apply.

The **Cleanverse Compliance Protocol (CCP)** connects these elements:

| Foundation | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Cleanverse Verified Identity · CVI** | Connects wallets with verified individuals and organisations. |
| **Cleanverse Verified Asset · CVA** | Establishes asset provenance through approved institutional pathways. |
| **Programmed Governance** | Applies eligibility and transfer rules before value moves. |

Together, they provide a foundation for identity-aware applications, controlled asset circulation, and traceable transfers across supported blockchain environments.

## Explore the Ecosystem

### Cleanverse — Infrastructure for Trusted Finance

A compliance-native framework connecting institutions, asset issuers, wallets, and infrastructure providers. Cleanverse brings identity verification, asset provenance, and policy enforcement into the flow of on-chain transactions.

[Discover Cleanverse →](https://cleanverse.com/)

### ClevrGo — Payments and AI Commerce

Built on Cleanverse, ClevrGo brings verified profiles, payment options, permissions, and transaction information into a connected experience for individuals and businesses.

Users can explore eligible payment routes and review relevant details before approval. Businesses can access merchant QR and POS options through participating providers, connect with customers and AI-led purchasing flows, and keep payment records together for tracking and reconciliation.

Availability depends on location, verification level, and provider requirements. Regulated financial services are delivered by participating providers.

[Explore ClevrGo →](https://clevrgo.com/)

### GovOS — Exploring Programmable Supervision

GovOS provides a beta environment for policymakers and compliance practitioners to explore how regulatory policies can translate into programmable controls within on-chain financial infrastructure.

## From Infrastructure to Real-World Use

The Cleanverse ecosystem supports a range of financial and commercial use cases:

- **Digital asset issuance** — Apply circulation policies to stablecoins, tokenised deposits, and tokenised real-world assets.
- **Verified value transfers** — Connect verified counterparties through identity-aware transaction flows.
- **Stablecoin access** — Support eligible funding and off-ramp pathways through participating institutions.
- **Merchant payments** — Connect digital asset payments with POS and smartphone experiences.
- **Embedded financial infrastructure** — Bring identity, asset verification, and transfer controls into partner applications.
